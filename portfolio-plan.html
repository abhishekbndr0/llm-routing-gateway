04 The projects

You can't finish three big things before fall. Do 1 + 2 — together they cover every row in the gap table, share a tech stack, and set you up for both SWE and AI roles.

~4–6 weeks

Your flagship. One project that covers five gaps at once. The move is to not leave it on your laptop — add the four wrappers so it becomes real "shipped" evidence. Build steps live in ROADMAP.md.

Priority 2

PyTorch model, served over FastAPI

~1–2 weeks

Closes the one gap the gateway can't. Train a small classifier on a non-toy dataset (skip MNIST — pick something with a story), then serve predictions behind a /predict endpoint and deploy it. "Trained a model and put it in production" is rare on a student résumé. Reuses the FastAPI skill, so it goes fast.
